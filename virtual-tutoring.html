VIRTUAL TUTORING

Quality Tutoring Without the Commute

Virtual tutoring has become an important part of TutorHelp. In fact, most of the tutoring requests we receive are for virtual sessions. Parents choose it because their child can work with a tutor from home without having to spend time driving to and from a tutoring center.

With a good internet connection, a computer or tablet and an interactive online session, virtual tutoring can provide the same individual attention, explanation and practice that a student would receive during an in-person session.

The other services we provide are also available with our virtual tutoring plans, including flexible scheduling, experienced tutors, homework help, regular tests and monthly progress reports. The goal is to make the virtual experience just as structured and supportive as our other tutoring options.

How We Maintain Our Virtual Sessions Interactive and Resourceful

Interactive Virtual 1:1 Sessions

Our virtual sessions are conducted one-on-one between the student and tutor. The student can ask questions, work through problems with the tutor, use the digital whiteboard and receive feedback throughout the session.

We don’t want the student to simply sit and watch a tutor explain a lesson. The session is designed to be bilateral, with the student actively answering questions, solving problems and discussing the concepts being taught.

Tools We Use

We use a combination of online tools to make lessons easier to explain and more engaging for students. Depending on the subject and lesson, tutors may use digital whiteboards, screen sharing, online worksheets, presentations and other educational resources during the session.

The tools may vary depending on what the tutor is teaching, but the purpose remains the same which is to make the virtual lesson interactive, clear and useful for the student.
