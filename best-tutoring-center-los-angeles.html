BEFORE YOU ENROLL

A simple parent checklist

  1. Ask what grades and subjects the provider supports.
  2. Ask whether tutoring is one-on-one or group-based.
  3. Ask how the tutor works with the student’s current school curriculum.
  4. Review pricing and the recommended session frequency.
  5. Look for real testimonials and information about the organization.
  6. Choose a provider whose format fits your child’s schedule and learning needs.

What makes a tutoring center one of the best in Los Angeles?

Parents should compare tutor qualifications, personalization, subjects, grade levels, pricing, scheduling, communication and evidence of student support—not just a star rating.
