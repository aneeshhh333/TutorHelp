SERVICES

Learning Shouldn’t End When the Lesson Ends

We don’t just conduct a lesson and move on. We make sure the student understands the concepts they are learning and can apply them on their own.

To help with this, we conduct regular bi-weekly or monthly tests and maintain a progress report for each student.

1. Personalized 1:1 Sessions

We believe a 60-minute 1:1 session gives students enough time to properly understand and work through a concept.

Our sessions are interactive, two-way, and enjoyable, rather than simply giving information to the student.

Students can have 1:1 sessions with an experienced tutor at our center or from the comfort of their home using a phone, laptop, or PC.

You can learn more about our virtual tutoring here.

2. Experienced Tutors

Every TutorHelp tutor has at least 5 years of tutoring experience and a postgraduate degree in their field of expertise. Every tutor at TutorHelp is also 28 years or older.

We conduct interviews to assess each tutor’s subject knowledge, teaching ability, and ability to adapt to different students’ needs.

After a short assessment of the student, we carefully match them with a tutor who fits their learning needs and goals.

3. Homework Help

Our tutors help students with their homework at the end of their sessions. But we don’t want students to simply get their homework done — we want them to develop the ability to solve problems on their own.

Tutors explain common patterns to look for in questions and teach students effective strategies for approaching and solving problems.

Note: Parents should submit the required school worksheets or papers to the tutor at least 2 hours before the upcoming session.

4. Curriculum Alignment

We teach concepts based on the curriculum followed by the student’s school and local school district. Our tests are also designed to closely match the concepts and material covered in the student’s school.

For this, the tutor may ask the parent to provide the student’s school textbook, previous tests, or exam papers.

Note: We also create personalized curricula for homeschooled students, students attending private or charter schools, and students living outside California.

5. Regular Tests

We conduct tests on a bi-weekly or monthly basis to evaluate the student’s understanding and progress.

These tests cover concepts that the student has learned during the previous two weeks or month. Test sessions are usually held on the 2nd and 4th Saturdays of each month.

The test sessions are included in the plan you purchase, so there is no additional fee. You can choose to have one or two tests per month. By default, both tests are included in the plan.

Test scores are calculated within 1–2 days. The tutor discusses the results with the student during the following session, while the test score, insights, and areas for improvement are included in the student’s monthly progress report.

Learn More

6. Monthly Progress Report

At the end of each month, we prepare a monthly progress report to help parents understand how their child is progressing.

For students taking online sessions, the report is sent to the parent by email at the end of the month.

The report is prepared by the student’s tutor and considers factors such as test scores during the month, attentiveness during sessions, topics the student is doing well in, and areas that may need additional attention.

7. Flexible Scheduling

We understand that plans can change and parents may sometimes need to reschedule a session.

At TutorHelp, our tutors are prepared to accommodate reasonable schedule changes. Parents should inform the tutor at least 24 hours before the scheduled session.

A convenient alternative date and time can then be selected for the rescheduled session.
