PRICING

Affordable Plans for Consistent Academic Support

We offer drop-in sessions and affordable discounted monthly plans for families looking for regular tutoring throughout the school year. Our plans are designed to make consistent academic support more manageable, while giving parents the flexibility to choose a plan based on how much support their child needs.

No one-time registration fee and no upfront yearly subscription. We encourage parents to pay as they go monthly for the service.

We also offer flexible payment plans, including monthly and bi-weekly payment options.
